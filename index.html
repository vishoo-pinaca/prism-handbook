    How a camera feed moves through PRISM

    Four ideas that explain most of PRISM

    1AI sits beside the video, never in it

    The part that moves video contains no AI. A detector can crash, restart or be upgraded and the cameras keep playing.

    2GPU memory is the budget

    Every AI camera and feature shares one graphics card’s memory. That is why AI is switched on per camera, and why each change is checked against capacity before it is applied.

    3People decide who is who

    PRISM names people already in its gallery. For anyone new, it collects and groups their faces, and an operator decides who they are.

    4Decide from the promises

    Nine promises, such as “live video never goes dark”, settle design arguments. A change that would break one goes back to the product owner. See all nine.
